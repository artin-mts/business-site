[Fig 1.0 — Questions]

Frequently asked questions

Do you need access to our ad account for the free breakdown?
No. The breakdown uses Meta's public Ad Library, so we can run it on your brand or any competitor without logins or ad account access.
How do you know what's "working" for competitors if the Ad Library doesn't show spend?
We infer it from signals brands can't fake for long: how many days an ad has been running, how many near-duplicate variants of a concept launch together (a sign it's being scaled), and whether a concept spreads across several brands. These are signals, not spend figures, and the brief labels them that way.
How is this different from Motion, Atria, or Foreplay?
Those are great tools, and the agent can work alongside them. The difference: we join every ad to your actual orders and margin, build the concept taxonomy around your brand, and run it in infrastructure you own, with no per-seat pricing.
What do you need from us for the pilot?
Read access to Meta Ads and Shopify (or your warehouse), a list of up to 5 competitors, and a 30-minute kickoff call. Most of the setup is on us.
Will the agent change our ads automatically?
Not unless you want it to. By default it recommends; your team decides what to act on.
Where does our data live? Is it secure?
In your own cloud warehouse (or one we set up in your account). We use read-only access and least-privilege credentials. Your data never leaves your infrastructure.
What if our data is a mess?
That's my background. If the foundation isn't ready, we fix the ads-to-orders join first as part of the build, or as a separate Data Foundation project.
Do you make the ads too?
No. We tell your team or agency what to make and why. Production stays with the people who are great at it.
