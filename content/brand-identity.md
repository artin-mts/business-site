# Brand Identity

## Overview

MTS Analytics is a boutique data consultancy for DTC brands. The brand is minimal, elegant, and quietly confident. No hype, no buzzword soup.

---

## Logo

**Concept:** Simple wordmark, horizontal layout

- "MTS" in semibold (600 weight)
- "Analytics" in light (300 weight)
- Neutral geometric sans-serif (system fonts or Inter)
- Monochrome: dark text on light background
- SVG format for scalability

**Usage:** Use on light backgrounds. For dark backgrounds, invert to white.

---

## Color Palette

**Primary Colors:**

- **Midnight:** `#1C1C1E` – Primary text, headings, dark sections
- **Soft White:** `#F5F5F7` – Background, light sections
- **Slate Blue:** `#526CFF` – Accent, CTAs, links, highlights
- **Cool Gray:** `#D5D7DC` – Borders, dividers, subtle lines
- **Black:** `#000000` – Strong headings (use sparingly)

**Usage Guidelines:**

- Use Midnight for 90% of text
- Use Soft White for backgrounds
- Use Slate Blue only for CTAs and key highlights
- Use Cool Gray for subtle separation
- Use Black sparingly for maximum impact

---

## Typography

**Primary Font:** Inter (system fallback: system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif)

**Weights:**

- **300 (Light):** For "Analytics" in logo, subtle text
- **400 (Regular):** Body text
- **500 (Medium):** Emphasized body text
- **600 (Semibold):** Headings, "MTS" in logo
- **700 (Bold):** Strong headings (use sparingly)

**Monospace:** System monospace for code-like text, tech stacks, metrics

**Scale:**

- Hero: 5xl–6xl (48–60px)
- Section Headings: 3xl–4xl (30–36px)
- Subheadings: 2xl (24px)
- Body: base (16px)
- Small: sm (14px)
- Tiny: xs (12px)

---

## Tone of Voice

**Principles:**

- **Minimal:** Short sentences, lots of whitespace
- **Clear:** No jargon unless necessary, then explain it
- **Confident:** State facts, don't oversell
- **Premium:** Quality over quantity
- **No fluff:** Every word earns its place

**Example Taglines:**

- "Modern data pipelines & AI-powered insights for DTC brands."
- "Build a world-class analytics foundation without hiring a full team."
- "Reliable, scalable ETL built the right way."
- "Your performance view in one place."

**What to Avoid:**

- ❌ "Revolutionary," "game-changing," "cutting-edge"
- ❌ Long paragraphs without breaks
- ❌ Excessive exclamation points
- ❌ Buzzwords: "synergy," "leverage," "disrupt"
- ❌ Overpromising or vague claims

**What to Use:**

- ✅ Specific, concrete benefits
- ✅ Short, punchy sentences
- ✅ Technical accuracy
- ✅ Real examples (anonymized)
- ✅ Clear calls to action

---

## Visual Style

**Layout:**

- Max-width containers (max-w-5xl)
- Generous padding and margins
- Lots of whitespace between sections
- Clean grid layouts (1, 2, 3, 4 columns as needed)

**Components:**

- Buttons: Slightly rounded (rounded-md), subtle hover states
- Cards: White background, subtle border, minimal shadow on hover
- Sections: Alternating backgrounds (softWhite/midnight) for rhythm

**Animations:**

- Subtle fade-ins (framer-motion)
- No parallax, no crazy effects
- Gentle, purposeful motion only
